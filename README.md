## 🎯 What it is

**Grabbit** is a small, self-hosted download service with one job and no nonsense. You paste a link — a public **Instagram Reel**, a **YouTube video**, a **Short** — pick video or audio, and the file lands in your downloads folder.

Every download site on the open web is a minefield of pop-ups, fake buttons and mystery redirects. Grabbit is the version you'd actually want: one page, one field, one file.

## ✨ What makes it pleasant

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🏷️ Files named like files
Your download is called **the name of the video** — spaces, accents and all — not `video_dQw4w9WgXcQ.mp4`.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔗 Many links at once
Add as many rows as you like. Each link saves as its **own file** — no archive to unzip afterwards — with a live "downloading 3 of 7" counter.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🍏 It plays on your phone
Files are delivered in a format Apple devices can actually open. A download that saves but won't play isn't a download — so compatibility wins over a bigger number on the resolution label.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🎧 Proper audio
Rip the sound on its own at **320 kbps MP3**, or keep the original audio track untouched.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔒 Yours only
A full-screen lock screen guards the app. Change the key and every device is signed out instantly. Leave it unset and it's open to anyone you share it with.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 📲 Install it
Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, with its own icon — on a phone, tablet or desktop.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🤖 It keeps trying
When a video is awkward, Grabbit works through alternative routes to it automatically. You see a file, not an error.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### ⚡ It fails fast when it should
A private, deleted or over-long video tells you so in a couple of seconds instead of spinning forever.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## 🎬 How it works, from your side

```
        ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │   1 · unlock          2 · paste your links      │
        │   3 · video or audio  4 · press the button      │
        └────────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                 ▼
                   ⏳  "downloading 2 of 5…"
                                 ▼
        🎞️  Reel.mp4     🎵  Song.mp3     🎞️  Short.mp4
              each saved separately, named after the video
```

<div align="center"><sub>That's the whole interface. There is no step five.</sub></div>
